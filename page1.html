#5 Pokemon Sword and Shield

Pokemon Sword and Shield (SWSH) is in 5th place because it was kinda the first open-world pokemon game, where in the whild area, you could explore with your bike, turn your camera around to view all sides, and be able to catch pokemon over-world.

Pokemon SWSH set the base line for all the Switch 1 games, going from a story-based game from Sun/Moon and Ultra Sun/Moon, SWSH was based more on catching pokemon and exploring th world with your buddies ratehr than ofcusing entirely on the story, though SWSH did have story aspects, it can be enjoyed from the eyes of a non-pokemon fan.

Pokemon SWSH is lower on this list because it doesnt have that much story once you beat the game. What I mean by is this game is the usual pokemon game, beat the elite four and the champions then the game is done. Someone who doesnt buy a DLC for games usually wont enjoy this game becasue it doesnt get good until you get the DLC and get the legendary pokemon the DLC gives you.
