#3 Pokemon Scarlet and Violet

Pokemon Scarlet and Violet (SV) is in 3rd place because it was another open-world game made by GameFreak, but this is entirely different from Pokemon Legends Arceus since it lets you choose which path to take first in the game. The different paths include different side characters for you to explore with. The first path is teh gyms an elite fours, the second path is the villian organization, and the third path is a frienzy pokemon battle you fight through to better know the side character. In the end, all of your friends and you explore the great crator in the middle of the map, where you learn the history of the region, depending on the version of the game, you will eiher catch the past pokemon or future pokemon. Not only was the main story amazing, the DLC introduced two new characters, and when both the main and LDC stories were finished, you got to go on one last mission with all your friends, which was wholesome and great to relive some of the nostalgia you probably had playing this game.

Pokemon SV introduces the climb, run, jump, swim, and glide feature that was presented in Pokemon Legends Arceus

The reason Pokemon SV is not higher up is it had too many bugs, and without the proper console, you at times couldn't play the game.
