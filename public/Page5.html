#1 Pokemon Legends ZA

In first place is pokemon legends za, the best game so far made by Nintendo and Gamefreak. The game is set around the future of kalos, similar to legends arceus, which was set in the past of Sinnoh. This game gave the option to roam freely across the map, with the more variety customization ever.

Pokemon Legends ZA intoduced the new battle mechanic, a real-time move that makes you dodge the opponent's attacks. This system can change the battle mechanics of the new pokemon games.

Pokemon Legends ZA is in first place because of the new battle mechanics, the new megas and old megas it had introduced, and the most fun DLC ever made, giving you over 360 pokemon to catch and add to your party in your adventure.
