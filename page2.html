#4 Pokemon Legends Arceus

Pokemon Legends Arceus is in 4th place because it was the actual first open-world pokemon game where you weren't restricted to one place until you beat the 'boss' of the area, though you did have to beat a boss to unlock mmore areas, the original area you were given is very big. This game focused more on the running around and catch your pokemon more than fighting gym leaders and beating the elite four. The game was more focused on learning the past of Sinnoh, named Hisue in the game, which lets the player explore the game and lore better

Pokemon Legends Arceus set the baseline for catching based games instead of fighting based games. It introduces new mechanics such as dodging incoming pokemon attacks and catching pokemon without battling it. This new mechanic sparked interests in pokemon fans as this game sold over 14.83 million copies. This mechanic copied over to GameFreak's next game, Legends ZA, who further made the mechanic better

Pokemon Legends Arceus isnt higher up in this list because the next three games are just better than this game, but Legends Arceus is still a good game played to this day.
